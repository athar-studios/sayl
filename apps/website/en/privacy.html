Privacy Policy · Sayl

Our privacy commitment is an architectural guarantee built directly into Sayl's design.

In Short

Sayl collects zero personal data. No accounts required, no telemetry, no tracking analytics, zero advertisements, and no intermediate proxy servers.

What Does the App Connect To?

Sayl connects exclusively to the hosts you explicitly request downloads from. Data packets flow directly between your computer and the target download server. No connection is ever routed through Athar Studio servers.

Locally Stored Data on Your Machine

Everything Sayl records stays strictly local on your device:

  • Settings & Task Journals: Saved inside Downloads\SAYL\.data.
  • This data belongs strictly to you and can be completely wiped at any time by deleting that folder.

This Website (sayl.site)

The sayl.site web portal consists of static HTML/CSS files hosted on Cloudflare Pages. It sets no tracking cookies and runs no telemetry scripts. Fonts and icons are 100% self-hosted.

External links (such as GitHub or Ko-fi) take you to their respective domains governed by their own policies.
