Summary: Which one is right for you?

If you value clean, source-available software, zero telemetry, no suspicious cracks or serial activations, native Arabic and English support, and rock-solid multi-threaded resumption — Sayl is the cleanest modern choice for Windows.

If your daily workflow heavily relies on floating video grabber bars on proprietary streaming sites today, note that Sayl's video extraction integration is arriving in v0.2.0.
