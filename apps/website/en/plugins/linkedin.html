LinkedIn Video Plugin

Save professional talks, presentations, and feed videos from LinkedIn for offline playback.

Feature Status: This plugin is part of our approved roadmap for the upcoming v0.2.0 release and is not present in v0.1.0. We are actively integrating open-source yt-dlp backends to deliver clean, fast, ad-free downloads.

Planned Capabilities:

  • Quality & Bitrate Selection: Select your preferred resolution from 360p up to 4K and 60fps where available.
  • Direct Audio Extraction: Isolate audio-only tracks (MP3 or M4A) to save storage and data bandwidth.
  • Batch & Playlist Downloads: Paste playlist URLs to queue complete series with structured metadata.
  • Segmented & Resumable Streams: Reliable byte-accurate checkpoints preventing failed long-format transfers.

Participate in Development

If you'd like to contribute code or test early prototypes of this plugin, follow our GitHub repository.
