Free Download Manager for Windows 10 & 11

If you struggle with slow browser downloads or broken multi-gigabyte files, Sayl is engineered to be the modern, reliable solution for Windows.

Why Browsers Struggle with Large Downloads

Web browsers (like Chrome, Edge, and Firefox) handle transfers over a single baseline connection. This leads to:

  • Suboptimal throughput: Single connections rarely saturate modern broadband speeds.
  • Corrupted transfers on dropouts: A brief Wi-Fi blip or system sleep often causes the browser to abort the entire file, forcing you to start from 0%.
  • Bloated resource footprint: Heavy downloads run alongside dozens of browser tabs.

How Sayl Solves It

Engineered from scratch in Rust, Sayl focuses entirely on fast, resilient downloading:

  1. Parallel Segmented Acceleration: Files are split into concurrent streams, maximizing throughput up to the server's bandwidth limits.
  2. Atomic Resumption (.part): Progress checkpoints are recorded atomically. If your network interrupts, Sayl validates ETag headers and resumes from the exact byte.
  3. Ultra-lightweight Rust Footprint: Consuming under 35MB of RAM, Sayl runs quietly in the system tray without burdening your CPU.
  4. Sleep Prevention & Auto-Shutdown: Prevents Windows from sleeping during active tasks, and can shut down your PC with a 30-second cancellation safety window.
